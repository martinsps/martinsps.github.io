The **Official College of Psychology of Western Andalusia** features an article
published in the journal **Apuntes de Psicología** on the application of
**artificial intelligence** to psychology, built around the idea of AI as a
**therapeutic copilot**.

The article contrasts "automated therapists" with **digital copilots** that
strengthen clinical judgment without replacing the professional relationship,
covering a range of applications —from assessment to documentation— and presenting
**PsyPilot by Medea Mind** as an example of this approach. It also addresses
governance challenges and ethical dilemmas, proposing a framework in which
technology **supports** clinical decisions. Its conclusion: the future of the
profession will depend on acquiring new competencies and **algorithmic literacy**,
rather than resisting technology.

The article is authored by **Pablo Roca Morales**, **Martín Sánchez-Pedreño**,
**Guillermo Rodríguez-Fernández**, **Eduardo P. García del Valle** and **Rosaria
Maria Zangri** (Villanueva University, Medea Mind and IE University).
