**Infocop**, the official media of the **General Council of Psychology of Spain**,
features the **NOVA project** in the article **"Can precision mental health
improve the outcomes of psychological treatments?"**.

The piece frames **precision mental health** as an approach that personalizes
psychological treatment through the **continuous evaluation of patient progress**
and the use of data and **artificial intelligence**, starting from a figure that
challenges the profession: almost half of patients do not achieve clinically
significant improvements, and dropout rates range between 30% and 40%.

The article positions the **NOVA (Navigating Outcomes via Analytics)** project as
an effort to **bridge the gap between research and clinical practice**, noting that
only 14% of professionals routinely monitor their patients' progress and that
fewer than 1% of the predictive models developed in research reach real-world
services. Throughout, clinical judgment remains the professional's responsibility,
supported by objective data that help detect stagnation or deterioration early.
